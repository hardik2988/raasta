About

Raasta turns a block number into a walking route, so anyone new to campus can find their way without asking.

A simple walking map for new students and visitors. Pick where you are, pick where you need to be, and follow one clear route with step-by-step directions.

Who it is for

Built for people who are new to the campus.

01 · Main audience

First-year students

Blocks are named by number, so a class in "Block 9" tells you nothing about where to walk. Raasta turns a number into a route.

02 · Occasional users

Visitors and parents

They arrive at the gate with no map in their head and need to find a hostel or the Management Development Centre quickly.

03 · Short-term users

Event volunteers

They guide other people. A clear route with distances lets them give directions without guessing.

How it works

Three steps, one path.

  1. 01

    Pick where you are

    Choose your starting place from the list or tap it on the map.

  2. 02

    Pick where you are going

    Choose a destination. The route appears straight away.

  3. 03

    Follow the route

    Read the steps, tick them off as you walk and watch the ring close when you arrive.

Reading the map

Colour, shape and letter tell you what a place is.

Every place of the same type shares one colour, one shape and one category letter, so you can scan the map quickly without reading every label.
